Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Moshly collects, why, who else handles it, and the rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is ContrastDetails Lda (NIF PT515858765), Rua Gonçalves Crespo 13, 1.º Dto, 2775-579 Carcavelos, Portugal. For anything about your data, write to hello@moshly.io.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and password. We store the password only in a hashed form.
  • Profile data: anything you choose to add, such as job title, organisation, location and bio.
  • Your content: what you create in the apps, such as projects, budgets, tasks, roadbooks and merch records.
  • Plan and billing data: your plan, its status and your usage of plan limits. If you buy a paid plan, our payment provider processes your payment details. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact form or by email.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic request information, used to keep the service secure, for example to stop repeated failed logins.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide Moshly and your account (performance of our contract with you).
  • To keep Moshly secure and prevent abuse (our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users).
  • To send you service emails, such as account verification, password resets and changes to your plan or to these policies (performance of contract).
  • To keep invoices and accounting records (legal obligation).
  • To answer your messages (our legitimate interest, or steps you asked for before a contract).

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

4. Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, under contracts that oblige them to protect it:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database and security.
  • Resend: sending service emails.
  • Our payment provider: processing payments, when you buy a paid plan.
  • AI model providers: only when you use an AI feature, and only the content you send to that feature.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. When they do, the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission or by the Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account data and content for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete them within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer. For example, invoicing records are kept for the period required by Portuguese tax law. Security logs are kept for a short time and then deleted.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use only what is strictly necessary to run Moshly: two cookies that keep you signed in (moshly_at and moshly_rt), and your browser's local storage for preferences such as light or dark mode. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, so we do not ask for cookie consent.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to hello@moshly.io and we will reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), or to the authority in the country where you live.

8. Children

Moshly is not meant for anyone under 16. If we learn that we hold data about someone under 16, we will delete it.

9. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, hashed passwords, short-lived sign-in tokens and access limited to the people who need it to run Moshly.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you by email or in your dashboard before the change takes effect.
