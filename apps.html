The kit

What goes in the van

Each app does one job, the one you'd otherwise fight a spreadsheet for. Use one, use all of them, same account.

  • Quote
  • MerchPad
  • Run
  • Rank
  • Days
  • FifthSense
  • Time

  1. Quote

    Live

    Budgets for gigs and tours

    Put in your fee, the gear, travel and crew, and get a clean budget you can send to the promoter as a PDF. No hunting for the right spreadsheet template the night before.

    Open Quote In paid plans
    • Rates, equipment and travel in one place
    • A PDF ready to send in minutes
    • Made for gigs, venues and tour planning

  2. MerchPad

    Live

    The merch table, sorted

    Big tap buttons for every shirt, record and tote, so whoever is behind the table can count sales after the show without thinking. Stock and restocks stay in view.

    Open MerchPad In paid plans
    • Big tally-style buttons, one tap per sale
    • Inventory, sales and restocks in one place
    • MerchPad 2: fewer screens, fewer taps

  3. Run

    Live

    The roadbook, on everyone's phone

    The tour in one place the whole crew can open: schedules, venues and logistics, from the first soundcheck to the last load-out. No more "which PDF is the latest one?"

    Open Run In paid plans
    • Schedules and venue details per date
    • Shared with the whole crew
    • Made for the phone, in the van

  4. Rank

    Live

    Get found online

    See how visible you are online and what to fix, so fans, promoters and press land on your music first. Plain words, no SEO course.

    Open Rank In paid plans
    • Your visibility at a glance
    • Compare with similar artists
    • Clear next steps, not jargon

  5. Days

    Live

    Tasks and dates

    The band's to-do list and calendar in one place: who's booking the van, when the artwork is due, what's left before the release.

    Open Days In paid plans
    • Tasks and schedules together
    • Synced through your Moshly account

  6. FifthSense

    Free

    The circle of fifths, interactive

    Keys, chords and how they relate, with visual feedback. Handy for writing, rehearsing or explaining a change to the rest of the band.

    Open FifthSense Free
    • Key signatures and chord relationships
    • Free for everyone, no paid plan needed

  7. Time

    Free

    A big clock for the side of the stage

    Fullscreen clock, timer, stopwatch and countdown to doors or curfew, with digits that fill the screen. Leave it on a laptop or phone where the band can see it.

    Open Time Free
    • Clock, timer, stopwatch and countdowns
    • Free for everyone, no account needed
